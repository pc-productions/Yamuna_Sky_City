We are pleased to share that Mangalore Meri Jaan, a prominent digital creator platform based in Mangaluru, has featured Yamuna Sky City in a recent Instagram post.

Mangalore Meri Jaan is known for capturing the essence of the city, from food and culinary experiences to lifestyle and events. Their feature offered a closer look at our flagship residential project: the tower at Kulai, its sea-facing homes and the amenities around them.

Yamuna Sky City, set in Kulai with the Arabian Sea in front of every apartment, is our idea of refined coastal living. We are grateful to Mangalore Meri Jaan for presenting the development to their audience and conveying our vision so well.

You can view the post on Instagram at [@mangaloremerijaanofficial](https://www.instagram.com/p/DQraCTeEmOA/).
